# Quote for a 42 m mesh fence (English interface)

You are a project engineer working in English. You are at your desk with the
site photos and the measurements the crew sent you.

## What the customer wants
A mesh fence 42 m long, with one corner roughly in the middle. The ground is
sloped — one end sits about half a metre lower. The customer wants a 3.5 m gate.

## What you have to produce
A final number you can send to the client today.

## How you work today
Photos and a hand sketch from the crew, laser measurements typed into Excel,
then a PDF quote by email. If this app is not faster than that, it is worth
nothing to you.

## When you give up
When you are stuck more than three times in the same place, or when you see a
screen that looks like code. Say so out loud and stop.
